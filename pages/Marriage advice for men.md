# Marriage advice for men

If you've been dating your girlfriend for two years I think you would benefit from spending one (full) hour considering asking her to marry you.

Yes, talk to her. And yes this isn't for everyone, but I think many men need to consider it seriously. 

So i'm gonna make the arguments:

## "We know we will be together forever"

Okay then, marry her! 

Also, she doesn't know this. I find that marriage tends to benefit female anxiety more. (On average)

## "I have to get money/house/job in order first"

In my experience you don't. I've been to weddings that cost $1k and weddings that cost $50k. The former were still great weddings.

## "I'm not sure we're there yet"

It's been two years. What new information are you gonna get? Is there some deeper concern? If so, what is it? Let's deal with it now, not in 6 years time. 

## "I would like to wait a bit"

Okay. It's your life. But remember her fertility window may be limited. Waiting should surely serve some purpose if it's costing her. 

## "Well she has been dropping hints.."

Is it your general practice to ignore when she asks for things? What is too soon for you might not be soon enough for her.

## "We're fine as we are"

Are you sure? Have you talked to married friends in your social circles? Many of mine seem really happy. Perhaps happier than they thought they'd be. 

If she weren't fine, would she actually tell you?

---

Look, I know everyone's life is different and I don't know yours. Communication is key.

I'm not super confident in this advice, but I do think it's underrated. 

Get better sleep, get some exercise, marry your long term girlfriend.
