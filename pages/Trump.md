## Key Topics

### Why Did People Vote for Trump?
- To what extent did people vote for Trump because they were tired of being down-trodden and wanted somebody who was seen as a winner? 
- And if so, to what extent is Trump going to win on the causes they care about? 

### Key Concerns - Not Dying
- **Not dying in a nuclear war**: Trump actually seems to want to avoid one of these. It remains to be seen whether he is successful at that. 
- **Not dying in an AI disaster**: This is very unclear to me. 
- **Not dying in a pandemic**: Defunding mRNA vaccine research seems very bad here. 

You might wonder why I chose these "not dying" options first. Well, I think many people wish not to die more than just about anything else. And so, whether people like it or not, their revealed preferences are that these things matter.
