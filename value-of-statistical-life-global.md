# Global Value of Statistical Life (VSL) Comparison

*Last updated: August 2025*

The Value of Statistical Life (VSL) represents the monetary value assigned to reducing the risk of death by one statistical life in cost-benefit analyses of public policies. This page compiles official government VSL estimates from around the world. It was largely written by Claude Code, but edited by me. I hope that I'll be able to [[factcheck]] it. 

## What is VSL?

VSL is not the value of an individual life, but rather the additional cost that individuals would be willing to pay for small reductions in risks that, in the aggregate, reduce the expected number of fatalities by one. It's a crucial parameter for evaluating safety regulations, environmental policies, and infrastructure investments.

## VSL by Country (2024 Values)

### United States
- **Department of Transportation (DOT)**: $13.7 million USD (2024) [Source](https://www.transportation.gov/office-policy/transportation-policy/revised-departmental-guidance-on-valuation-of-a-statistical-life-in-economic-analysis)
- **Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)**: ~$10-12 million USD (2024 adjusted from $7.4M in 2006) [EPA Mortality Risk Valuation](https://www.epa.gov/environmental-economics/mortality-risk-valuation)
- **Department of Health and Human Services**: $12.5 million USD (2024) [HHS Standard Values](https://aspe.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/documents/cd2a1348ea0777b1aa918089e4965b8c/standard-ria-values.pdf)
- **Consumer Product Safety Commission**: Updated guidance issued April 2024 [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/04/18/2024-08300/notice-of-availability-of-final-guidance-for-estimating-value-per-statistical-life)

### United Kingdom
- **Department for Transport (DfT)**: £1.8 million GBP (~$2.3 million USD) [Source: Bristol University Research](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/publications/what-is-the-value-of-life-a-review-of-the-value-of-a-prevented-fa)
  - Criticized as significantly undervalued; researchers suggest £16-22 million [Bristol News](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/news/2019/october/flaws-in-the-uk-vpf.html)
- **NICE (Healthcare)**: [NICE Cost-effectiveness](https://www.nice.org.uk/news/blogs/should-nice-s-cost-effectiveness-thresholds-change-)
  - Standard: £20,000-30,000 per QALY
  - End-of-life care: £50,000 per QALY
  - Very rare diseases: £100,000-300,000 per QALY

### Australia
- **Office of Impact Analysis**: $5.7 million AUD (2024) [Australian Government OIA](https://oia.pmc.gov.au/resources/guidance-assessing-impacts/value-statistical-life)
- **Value of Statistical Life Year (VLY)**: $245,000 AUD (2024) [Source](https://oia.pmc.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-10/value-of-statistical-life.pdf)

### Canada
- **Treasury Board**: Specific 2024 value not publicly available [Policy on Cost-Benefit Analysis](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/laws/developing-improving-federal-regulations/requirements-developing-managing-reviewing-regulations/guidelines-tools/policy-cost-benefit-analysis.html)
- **Transport Canada**: Uses Treasury Board standardized value [2019 Social Costs](https://tc.canada.ca/en/road-transportation/statistics-data/statistics-data-road-safety/2019-statistics-social-costs-collisions-canada)
- Note: Value contained in restricted Cost-Benefit Analysis Guide

### Japan
- **Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT)**: ¥210 million (~$1.4 million USD) [Based on search results - official source not directly accessible]

### European Union
- **European Commission (2024)**: €3.6 million base value for EU [EUROCONTROL Standards](https://ansperformance.eu/economics/cba/standard-inputs/latest/chapters/value_of_a_statistical_life-vsl.html)
- **Germany**: €4.1 million
- **France**: €3.8 million
- **Italy**: €3.3 million

### New Zealand
- **Treasury**: $4.9 million NZD (2020 - latest available) [BERL Analysis](https://berl.co.nz/our-mahi/if-not-benefit-cost-ratios-then-what)
- Updated CBAx tool released October 2024 for Budget 2025 [Treasury CBAx](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/information-and-services/public-sector-leadership/investment-management/investment-planning/treasurys-cbax-tool)

### Developing Countries

#### China
- **2024 Estimate**: CNY 5.33 million (~$750,000 USD) [Cambridge Study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-benefit-cost-analysis/article/building-a-set-of-internationally-comparable-value-of-statistical-life-studies-estimates-of-chinese-willingness-to-pay-to-reduce-mortality-risk/F0736C663CC8C30263752C6E7D187526)
- Range: CNY 3.79-6.36 million ($549,000-$922,000 USD) [ScienceDirect Study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2325426223000530)

#### India
- **2018 Estimate**: INR 44.69 million (~$640,000 USD) [Hedonic Wage Study](https://ideas.repec.org/p/sch/wpaper/407.html)

#### Brazil

#### Thailand

## Non Country values

- GiveWell estimates that the cost to save a human life is approximately $5,000.

## Key Observations

1. **Income Effect**: VSL values correlate strongly with national income levels
   - US VSL is ~20x higher than India's
   - Income elasticity: 0.55 for wealthy countries, 1.0 for low-income countries [[Cambridge Meta-Analysis]](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-benefit-cost-analysis/article/value-of-statistical-life-a-metaanalysis-of-metaanalyses/BC4015650AC911691EB91AAFD3AEBBFA)

2. **Methodology Matters**: Different approaches yield different values
   - Revealed preference (wage-risk studies)
   - Stated preference (contingent valuation)
   - Benefits transfer from other countries

3. **Policy Applications**: VSL is used for:
   - Transportation safety investments
   - Environmental regulations
   - Healthcare resource allocation
   - Occupational safety standards

4. **Recent Trends**:
   - Many countries haven't updated VSL in years (e.g., US EPA since 1997) [[RFF Analysis]](https://www.resources.org/common-resources/rethinking-the-value-of-a-statistical-life/)
   - Growing calls for harmonization within regions (e.g., EU)
   - Debate over appropriate values for global issues like climate change
